# The Implant and Oral Surgery Centre — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

The Implant and Oral Surgery Centre (IOSC) is a multidisciplinary dental practice at Novena Medical Center, Singapore. It offers implant treatment, oral and maxillofacial surgery, orthodontics and general dental care to local and international patients.

The redesigned homepage clarifies the clinic’s treatment focus, makes services easier to explore and provides direct routes to consultation enquiries.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. A Clear, Stable Opening Message

A descriptive H1 identifies implant and oral surgical care in Novena, supported by a persistent consultation action. This replaces four rotating promotional slides and addresses the previous absence of an H1, giving visitors a consistent introduction to the practice.

### 2. Easier Treatment Discovery

Grouped treatment pathways provide concise explanations and direct service links within the homepage body. Previously, visitors relied heavily on a Services menu containing 16 entries. The new overview helps patients identify relevant care before exploring individual treatment pages.

### 3. More Direct Contact

Visible clinic contact details, click-to-call links and persistent mobile contact actions make enquiries easier to initiate. These address the previous lack of telephone and email links on the homepage and reduce the steps required to contact the clinic.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** A single opening message prioritises treatment focus, location and the consultation action.
- **Layout:** Grouped treatment pathways create a structured, scannable service overview.
- **Consistency:** Clear messaging and descriptive service links support a coherent journey towards making an enquiry.
- **Mobile experience:** Persistent contact actions remain accessible while visitors browse.
