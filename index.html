Before a treatment decision

Start with questions.
Build understanding.

Your consultation is the place to discuss the concern, the assessment and the options available to you.

Contact the clinic

  1. Share your concerns

    Tell the clinic what brings you in, and mention relevant medical history or dental records when arranging the visit.

  2. Discuss the assessment

    Ask which examinations or investigations are needed, and what the findings mean for your treatment choices.

  3. Understand the next steps

    Discuss the proposed approach, alternatives, treatment stages, risks, recovery and estimated costs before deciding.

An enquiry is the first step. Contact the clinic to discuss appointment availability and confirmation. This page does not provide an instant booking service.

Helpful before you enquire

Know what to ask.
Know where to start.

For advice about your own symptoms or treatment suitability, arrange an assessment with the clinic.

Do I need to know which treatment I need?
No. Explain your concern when contacting the clinic. An assessment helps establish suitable options and whether further investigations are needed.
Can I discuss feeling nervous about surgery?
Yes. Tell the clinic about your concerns and ask about the anaesthetic options appropriate to your treatment and medical history.
How do I find out about fees?
Contact the clinic about consultation charges and the proposed treatment estimate. Ask which stages or additional procedures are included.
Can I ask about MediSave or CHAS?
The practice publishes information about financial options. Ask the clinic to confirm current eligibility, applicable procedures and claim conditions for your circumstances.
Can the clinic assist international patients?
IOSC publishes international-patient coordination services. Contact the clinic before travelling to discuss appointments and any assistance you require.
